This role requires a strong ownership mindset, the ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues, and a focus on delivering secure, reliable, and scalable solutions within a financial services environment.

● Apply security, reliability, and performance best practices appropriate for financial services applications.
● Collaborate with development, infrastructure, and business teams to deliver reliable technical solutions.
